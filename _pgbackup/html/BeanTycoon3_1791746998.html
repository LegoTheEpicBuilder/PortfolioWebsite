Bean Tycoon 3

What is it about?

In Bean Tycoon 3, you and up to five other players run your own bean production empire. Start small, expand your factory, and grow your business from the ground up to become the wealthiest man alive. Manage your production line as your beans are packed into cans, crates, and pellets. Expand your factory, claim regions for extra income and sabotage your rivals with weapons like the Bean Sword, Bean Gun, or Bean Bomb.

Bean Tycoon 3

Using machines

Players can purchase a variety of machines, each functioning slightly differently from the standard 'upgrader'. The Bean Machine, for example, requires a specific input. Once it receives enough resources, it begins processing for a few seconds before releasing the finished product at the back of the machine.

Building your tycoon

Expanding your factory requires money, which players earn by selling beans. Once you’ve saved up enough, you can purchase new machines and upgrades by pressing the green buttons scattered around your tycoon.

Rivalry

The other players are your rivals, and it’s about time you do something about it. Fortunately, there are several (perhaps ridiculous) weapons you can acquire, such as the Bean Sword, Bean Gun, and the explosive Bean Bomb.
